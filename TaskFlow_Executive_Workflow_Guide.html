2. Core Business Workflows

A. Project Delivery & Execution Lifecycle

How a project transitions from client contract to completion and billing:

1

Client & Project Creation (CEO / PM)

A client profile is created with currency and billing terms. A project is initiated (Name, Description, Target Deadlines). Note: Assigning team members is optional at inception.

2

Work Breakdown Structure (PM)

Project roadmap is decomposed into granular Tasks and Subtasks with assigned priorities (Critical, High, Medium, Low), estimated hours, and assigned engineers.

3

Task Execution & Live Updates (Developers)

Engineers move task states (Pending ➔ In Progress ➔ Ready for QA) and link their Git branches/commits.

4

Daily Timesheet Filing & Verification (Developer ➔ PM)

Engineers record hours spent on tasks tagged as Billable or Non-billable. PM verifies submitted hours against daily punch duration and clicks Approve.

5

Quality Assurance & Bug Regression (QA Engineer)

QA runs test cases. If a defect occurs, a Bug Ticket is filed and reassigned to developer. Once resolved, QA marks it Verified Complete.

6

Invoicing & Financial Realization (CEO / Accounts)

Approved billable hours are compiled with 1-click into branded PDF invoices and sent to the client. Realized revenue is tracked on the CEO dashboard.

3. Workforce Governance & Shift Architecture

TaskFlow features an automated IST (Asia/Kolkata) attendance engine that enforces workplace discipline, eliminates manual timekeeping, and automatically feeds into the monthly payroll module.

Automated Shift Timeline & Penalty Rules

Time Window (IST) Event / Trigger System Action & Compliance Rule Attendance Status
09:30 AM – 10:00 AM Early Arrival Window Clock-in allowed up to 30 mins before official shift start. Employee selects Office or WFH. On Time
10:00 AM – 10:30 AM Official Grace Period Standard 30-minute grace window for daily commuting variations. Present (Grace)
10:31 AM – 12:00 PM Late Arrival Registers a Late Instance. Up to 3 late instances allowed per month. Exceeding 3 instances automatically deducts a First Half Day. Late Flag
After 12:00 PM Excessive Delay Automatic conversion to Half-Day presence regardless of past late quota. Half Day
During Shift Break Governance Lunch Break (40m) & Tea Break (20m). Timed with live stopwatch on dashboard. Overages flagged as Excess Breaks. Monitored
19:00 PM Onwards Shift Completion Employee clocks out. System verifies net hours worked (Total elapsed minus excess breaks). Completed
11:00 PM Auto-Lock Forgotten Punch Out Shift auto-terminated. Employee cannot clock in next morning without submitting a Second Half Regularization Leave. Auto-Closed

Leave Management & Quota Structure

Employees are allocated fixed yearly allowances. Every leave follows an automated approval chain:

  • Casual Leave (CL - 12 Days): Planned personal time off. Requires prior PM approval.
  • Sick Leave (SL - 8 Days): Health emergencies; medical certificates attachable.
  • Paid Leave (PL - 15 Days): Earned leave accumulated across project service.
  • Short Leave (2 Hours): Allows stepping away for urgent matters without forfeiting half-day pay.
